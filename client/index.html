Step 1

Upload your report

Personal details are removed first. The original file is held in memory only long enough to read the test rows, then it is dropped. Nothing about you is saved on the server.

Drop a synthetic report here

Drop a PDF or photo from the test pack, or choose a PNG or JPEG of your own. A test-pack file is recognised by its name. Only the printed results are used. Names and NHS numbers are not read.

      Step 2

      Check what CarePath read

      Only test names, numbers, units, and ranges are shown. A name, NHS number, address, or date of birth is not in this preview. Correct anything the reader got wrong. Nothing is explained or stored until you tick the box.

      Age band

      CarePath does not ask for a date of birth, and it does not guess an age from a name.

      Step 4

      Your results, in plain language

      A fixed rule chooses the range. The explanation under each result comes from CarePath's patient library, not from a guess. This is not a diagnosis.

      Ask a question

      The model runs on CarePath's server and may answer only from retrieved passages. If it cannot, you get one fixed message. Sources are shown, then not saved.

        Ready for your GP

        Download a one-page summary with the results, the range that was used, and questions you could ask. It has no personal details, and it is not uploaded.
